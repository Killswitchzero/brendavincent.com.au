My Story

The Beginning

As a Television Commercial Producer, Personal Manager and Music Artist Manager my clients include just about every brand from Coca-Cola to L'Oreal alongside Australia and USA's top directors. Actors Sam Neill, Rachel Griffiths, Members from U2, Blvd of Eyes, Phil Emmanuel, Madonna, Prince, Midnight Oil, The Angels, Steve Williams with DIO's Craig Goldy, to Australian Rose Tattoo, and many more. Right hand to Grammy nominated Manager Chris Murphy, Personal Manager of INXS Management and Producer Sam Evans. We launched the first global digital record label for Universal Music Group. Chris easily taught me invaluable knowledge and strategy about the Music Industry.

The Now

As we enter the Advanced Intelligence era, I have learned and studied all the advantages, everything from Production and PR Schedules, budgets to Financials. It is essential that everyone understands how advantageous this is. I do not use AI for my imagery, writing or recording, but key for those music producers/bands it allows us less time on paperwork and more time for what I love, looking after my client's best interests.

My Philosophy

I believe that music has the power to bring people together and create unforgettable experiences. My approach is to always look after my clients, my business, with integrity and grit. I approach every event with this philosophy in mind, ensuring that every detail is perfect.
