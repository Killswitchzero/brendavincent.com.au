My Story

Artists Sam Henderson, Rachel Griffiths, Members from U2, Blvd of Eyes, Offsite Traffic, Madonna, Prince, Midnight Oil, Dave Steel, Steve Williams with Delta Craig Moody, to Australian Rose Tattoo, Phil Emmanuel and many more. Right hand to Grammy nominated Manager Chris Murphy, Personal Manager of INXS Management. We launched the first global digital record label for Universal Music Group. Chris easily taught me invaluable knowledge and strategy about the Music Industry.
